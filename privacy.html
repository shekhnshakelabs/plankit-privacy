PLANKIT & YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

A clear explanation of how the Plankit task management app handles information and reminders.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Plankit is a task management application designed to help you organize short-term tasks and long-term plans. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information and task storage

Plankit does not require you to create an account. Tasks and plans you create in the app are stored on your device. Plankit does not sell your personal information.

Notifications and reminders

If you choose to use reminders, Plankit may request Android notification permission and use your selected reminder settings to schedule notifications. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

Sharing information

Plankit does not intentionally share the contents of your tasks with third parties. If additional online services are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Managing your data

You may edit or remove tasks using the app's available controls. App information stored on your device may also be affected by Android's backup and restore settings. For questions about your data, please contact us at the email below.

Data security

We aim to protect information handled by Plankit. The security of locally stored information also depends on the security settings of your Android device.

Children's privacy

Plankit is a general-purpose planning app and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. A revised version will be published on this page with a new update date.

Contact us
